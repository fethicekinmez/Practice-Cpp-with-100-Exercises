Question: Write a C++ program to use bitwise operators left shift and right shift.


Expected output: The program should output the original number, the number after left shift and the number after right shift.


Original Number: 10
Number after left shift: 40
Number after right shift: 5

Hint:

Left shift operator (<<) shifts the bits of a number to the left by a specified number of positions, while the right shift operator (>>) shifts the bits of a number to the right by a specified number of positions
