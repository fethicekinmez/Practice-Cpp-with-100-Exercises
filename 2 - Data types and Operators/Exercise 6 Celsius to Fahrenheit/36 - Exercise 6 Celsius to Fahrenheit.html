Question: Write a C++ program to convert temperature from Celsius to Fahrenheit.


Expected output: The program should take input from the user in Celsius and output the temperature in Fahrenheit.


Enter temperature in Celsius: 25
Temperature in Fahrenheit: 77

Hint:

To convert temperature from Celsius to Fahrenheit, you can use the formula F = (C * 9/5) + 32, where F is the temperature in Fahrenheit and C is the temperature in Celsius.
