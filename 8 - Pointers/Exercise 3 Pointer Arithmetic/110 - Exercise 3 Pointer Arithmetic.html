Question: Write a C++ program to find the sum of two numbers using pointers.

Expected output: The program should output the sum of the two numbers.


Enter first number: 10
Enter second number: 20
The sum of 10 and 20 is: 30

Hint:

Declare two integer variables and two pointers to integers. Assign the addresses of the variables to the pointers and use the pointers to add the values of the variables.
